# Dual-Path Research Progression Contract v1

Last materially modified: 2026-08-04

Every frozen research gate now has two independent continuation routes.

## Hard-Gate Path

The original Spec, thresholds, report, and pass/fail result remain immutable. A passing identity may eventually receive `research_supported_promotion` after its downstream economic, execution, transport, and safety gates pass.

## Owner-Progression Path

The owner may continue Development work after a hard-gate failure. The successor must bind the failed Spec and report hashes, enumerate every accepted risk, remain marked outcome-informed, and state exactly what next stage is being opened. It may not relabel the predecessor as passed.

Owner progression is not permanently diagnostic-only. It may eventually receive `owner_risk_accepted_promotion`, but only after a separate full-path economic experiment demonstrates improvement and the same execution parity, tail, safety, and promotion-controller requirements are satisfied. The final live identity must preserve the owner-risk label.

## Evidence Asymmetry And Stop Rule

The two paths are governance routes, not equivalent evidence classes. A normal hard-gate pass supports `research_supported_promotion`. Owner progression only records that the owner accepted a disclosed failure in order to gather new Development evidence; it never inherits the failed gate's evidentiary claim.

If the owner continuation produces no executable candidate, it stops at that research layer. It may not open an action experiment merely because the owner route exists. Any later owner promotion requires a genuinely new full-path identity with positive economic evidence, execution parity, and safety support, while permanently retaining both the original failure and the `owner_risk_accepted_promotion` label.

Registration alone never grants Validation, holdout, action, or live authority.
