## Mmap Lifetime Rule

When any one-shot arm or result consumer raises, the scheduler cancels every not-yet-started sibling and waits for every running sibling future to terminate before the read-only day mmap may close. Real two-thread failure-injection tests cover both arm failure and result-consumer failure while a sibling remains inside the mapped lifetime.

## Semantic-Safe Completed-Side Resume
