# Causal Multichannel Boolean Cooldown Persistent Policy V3 Design

Date: 2026-08-12

Identity: `causal_multichannel_window_boolean_cooldown_persistent_policy_v3`

Status: design only. No successor outcomes have been read, no unified policy is frozen, and no action or live authority exists.

## Why This Is A New Identity

The predecessor produced two separate facts:

1. Historical strict labels were blocked by non-identifiable event ordering.
2. A limited modeled-queue one-shot search did not pass outer OOF.

The predecessor did not evaluate the full feature hierarchy, a broad ordered multi-rule Boolean class, or repeated-policy economics. Expanding those objects after seeing predecessor outcomes is outcome-informed design, so it cannot be presented as a rerun of the old preregistration.

The consumed 40-day panel may be used as training material for this explicitly outcome-informed successor. It may not be reused as fresh outer support.

## Required Hierarchy

The successor must implement, rather than merely document:

\[
M0\ absolute
\rightarrow
(M1-M0)\ paired
\rightarrow
(M2-M1)\ paired
\rightarrow
(continuous-Boolean)\ paired
\]

M1 or M2 cannot advance because its independent absolute cell happens to pass. BUY and SELL remain separate. The simultaneous family includes side and every frozen hierarchical comparison.

## Search Boundary

Large blocks may no longer be represented as though a 32-predicate hash subset were the full architecture. Predicate selection may occur inside chronological training folds, but never on outer-test outcomes. The execution amendment must freeze compute budgets before successor outcome access and must make ordered multi-rule candidates reachable in practice.

The continuous comparator measures information lost by Boolean thresholding. It does not silently replace the requested Boolean policy.

## Distribution And Censoring

Every outer fold must report side, role, consecutive units, UTC hour, censoring, predicate prevalence, three-state missingness, SMD, PSI, and both equal-day and campaign-weighted estimates. A sign that changes under day weighting is not stable promotion evidence.

The preferred label carries D+1 or continuous state to joint washout. If that is unavailable, partial-identification bounds must be frozen before outcomes. Complete-case deletion and a newly invented terminal timeout are forbidden.

## Evidence Lanes

Research-supported evidence requires order-identified receive-time or equivalent strict queue inputs. A modeled-queue lane may continue only as a permanently labelled owner exploration. The two lanes cannot be pooled, and a modeled-queue result cannot acquire live authority without independent order path and transport evidence.

Only a supported, unified side policy can enter repeated-policy replay. That stage must then run BUY-only, SELL-only, joint 50-day full-path A/B, restart-aware continuous confirmation, and receive/feature-ready transport before any bounded live canary is considered.
