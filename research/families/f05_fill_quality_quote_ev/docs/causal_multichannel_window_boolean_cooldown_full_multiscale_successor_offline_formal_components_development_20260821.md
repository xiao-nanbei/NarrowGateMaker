# Full-Multiscale Cooldown Successor Formal Components

Last materially modified: 2026-08-21

Question: does the frozen full-multiscale cooldown learner improve sequential campaign economics over the exact current owner policy on the 30-day historical Development panel?

Result: the separately executed BUY and SELL nested out-of-fold (OOF) components are complete and have been joined by an identity-only cross-commit composition receipt. ## BUY Result

BUY E3 was the strongest economic point result:

| Metric | Exact owner | BUY E3 | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Terminal value | -2.904117 USDC/day | -2.445540 USDC/day | +0.458577 USDC/day; loss reduced 15.79% |
| Closed-campaign value | -3.018197 USDC/day | -2.414185 USDC/day | +0.604012 USDC/day; loss reduced 20.01% |
| Fills | 8,565 | 8,333 | -232; down 2.71% |

The gross terminal uplift was positive in all four outer folds and on 13 of 20 OOF test days. The action-matched control reduced fills slightly more, from 8,565 to 8,312, but improved terminal value by only +0.264689 USDC/day. E3 therefore added +0.193888 USDC/day beyond the action-matched control without buying that increment through a larger fill sacrifice. This is a promising Development execution-selection alpha signal.

It is not a promotion result. The simultaneous day band for E3's gross terminal uplift was `[-0.348400, +1.265554]` USDC/day, while the four-week-block band was `[+0.140288, +0.917819]`. The semantic increment over the action-matched control also had an unstable day band of `[-0.715169, +1.102945]` USDC/day. The frozen hierarchy's first E1-versus-B0 step failed, so later E2/E3 steps were not confirmatorily admitted. E3 also failed required tail and lifecycle gates. Its scorecard therefore remains `development_failed_family_closed`, with no ranking or action authority.

## SELL Result

SELL had no supported non-baseline policy. E3 was the least negative enriched candidate at -0.009708 USDC/day relative to the exact owner, retained 101.18% of fills, and improved on 12 of 20 OOF test days, but its simultaneous lower bound was negative and its tail and lifecycle gates failed. E1, E2, M2, the continuous comparator, and the simpler campaign-age candidate also had negative terminal point increments. The hierarchy supported no SELL feature block, and all 13 SELL scorecards were ineligible for ranking.

## Decision

The BUY E3 point result is worth preserving as a high-priority future confirmation candidate because it reduced modeled losses materially while retaining 97.29% of fills and added value beyond an action-matched control. The present evidence does not justify production capital, a live action, or a post-outcome rewrite of the frozen gate. Any future confirmation must freeze E3 and its thresholds before reading new evidence and must preserve the current Validation and sealed-holdout boundaries.

SELL supplies no successor candidate. The active owner-risk-accepted SELL cooldown policy is unchanged, and this formal result must not be used to relabel that separate live exception as research-supported.
