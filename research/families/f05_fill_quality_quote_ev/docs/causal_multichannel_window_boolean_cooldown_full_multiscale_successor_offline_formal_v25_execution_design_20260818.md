# F05 Formal-v25 SELL Resume Design

Last materially modified: 2026-08-18

Status: `formal_v25_implemented_local_pre_admission`.

## Question

Formal-v25 asks whether formal-v24 can be resumed without recomputing its completed BUY side after repairing the mmap lifetime race, while preserving the exact frozen data, candidate ladder, folds, estimand, statistics, B0 control, and false action/live permissions.

## Frozen Research Contract

Formal-v25 preserves the formal-v24 30-day source manifest, 3,516 mechanics opportunities and ordering, exact current-owner B0 policy and predicate bundle, eight side-specific duration arms, candidate ladder, four-by-three chronological folds, sequential repeated-policy estimand, `action_alpha_v1` statistics, and evidence boundaries. It is an execution-only successor and does not reopen model, predicate, duration, threshold, split, or day selection.

## Lifetime Repair

When any one-shot arm or result consumer raises, the scheduler must cancel every not-yet-started sibling and wait for every running sibling future to terminate before the day projection context may close its read-only mmap. A real two-thread failure-injection test holds one sibling on the mapped lifetime while another fails and proves that the exception is re-raised only after the sibling exits.

## Completed-Side Resume

Formal-v25 may inherit exactly 577 completed BUY cache units from formal-v24: 67 outer-train one-shot, 250 inner-OOF, and 260 outer-OOF. Each successor key is derived from its predecessor by changing only the adapter artifact SHA and execution-manifest SHA. The source manifest, panel manifest, fold manifest, exact owner policy, candidate policy, side, stage, fold, UTC day, and day-input hash must match byte-for-byte.

Before C++ qualification or formal economics, a zero-economic cache census must validate every predecessor progress receipt, cache manifest, and payload byte hash. It must also prove that the ten SELL outer1 `running` receipts have no admitted entries. Missing or drifted BUY material stops the run; BUY recomputation and reuse of any incomplete SELL material are forbidden.

## Execution Order

Formal-v25 must be bound to a new clean commit and annotated tag, admit a v24-to-v25 research-contract invariance receipt, admit the completed-BUY cache census, rerun the all-panel zero-economic C++ builder gate, rerun first-opportunity and full-day Python/C++ lockstep qualification, and pass the formal mechanics preflight. Only then may nested OOF start.

The invariance receipt separately binds byte-identical research-semantic sources and the four changed execution-only sources: the replay adapter, repeated-policy backend, formal orchestrator, and C++ qualification runner. Every execution-only source must differ from v24 and carry both predecessor and successor SHA256 values; this allowance does not extend to feature, candidate, estimand, or statistical code.

During nested OOF, the learning algorithm reconstructs BUY folds from hash-verified predecessor caches and executes no BUY replay. SELL begins from outer1 one-shot and uses the repaired ten-thread C++ arm pool. Outer-test sequential repeated-policy replay remains Python-authoritative.

## Stop Rules

Any cache census mismatch, inherited-key mismatch, mmap failure-injection regression, C++ qualification mismatch, source or panel drift, incomplete scorecard, or permission drift fails closed. A failed successor is preserved under its own identity; another execution version is not started automatically.

Formal-v25 does not deploy or modify EC2, create a shadow or companion, modify the active owner policy, read Validation or sealed holdout, expose intermediate economics, or grant action/live authority.
