## Change From v4

v5 retained the v4 model features, denominator, probability simplex, empirical report cuts, outer chronological folds and byte-identical curve gate. The only change was the calibration procedure inside each outer train:

```text
outer train
  -> earlier core-fit days: fit common side-specific fill/cancel hazards
  -> final five train-only days: fit opener/add/reducing cause offsets
  -> outer test: apply only offsets estimated from prior days
```

Calibration days were excluded from the core model fit. Outer-test rows never entered fitting or calibration. Missing role or cause support had no fallback and would fail closed.

## Result

All probability and monotonicity identities passed, all six curves had adequate support, and all joint/fill/cancel Brier-improvement lower bounds remained positive. The unchanged gate nevertheless passed only two of six curves:

| Curve | v4 cancel bias | v5 cancel bias | v5 95% interval | v5 result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| BUY add | -0.00364 | -0.00332 | [-0.01135, +0.00525] | pass |
| BUY opener | -0.00755 | -0.01071 | [-0.01701, -0.00460] | fail |
| BUY reducing | -0.00957 | -0.00752 | [-0.01356, -0.00135] | fail |
| SELL add | +0.00447 | +0.00047 | [-0.00952, +0.01028] | pass |
| SELL opener | -0.00772 | -0.01033 | [-0.01728, -0.00405] | fail |
| SELL reducing | -0.01111 | -0.00797 | [-0.01510, -0.00055] | fail |

Role calibration improved both reducing biases and centered SELL add, but it worsened both opener biases. The score improvements were nearly unchanged. This is redistribution of calibration error rather than stable transfer.

## Interpretation

The five-day role offsets are causal and role-aware, but still static over the future outer test. Baseline cancel ACK is largely generated by deterministic or state-dependent policy clocks:

- scheduled requote/TTL request time;
- role-dependent replacement behavior;
- activation and cancel-ACK latency;
- fills during the request-to-ACK race;
- changing order age and pending-cancel state.

A static role intercept cannot fully transport that process across days. v5 therefore rejects the narrow hypothesis that side-by-role hazard-level offsets alone solve cancel-CIF calibration.

The next defensible options are materially different families:

1. inner-expanding OOF calibration using multiple past calibration folds rather than one trailing five-day block; or
2. model cancel request as the known policy stopping rule and model only the request-to-ACK/fill race statistically.

Neither option may reuse the v5 family identity or read v5 Validation.

The first option was subsequently implemented as the separately frozen v6 inner-expanding family. It also failed Development: all six cancel-ACK curves were underpredicted after the inner-OOF maps were transferred to full outer-train refits. See `docs/placement_fill_nested_role_competing_cif_v6_development_20260727.md`.
