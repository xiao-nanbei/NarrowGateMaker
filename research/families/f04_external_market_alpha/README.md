The preregistered receive-time study is [`first_add_external_incremental_value_m0_m1_v1`](docs/first_add_external_incremental_value_m0_m1_v1_preregistration_20260730.md). It compares campaign-state plus local causal microstructure against the same model with Bitget/Bybit/OKX receive-time state, using F10's direct first-add decision-to-campaign-terminal USDC target. Every chronological, true leave-one-venue-out, quality-panel, lifecycle, common-row, causal-clock, and late-panel denominator must pass; older future-direction and maker-lifecycle screens cannot substitute for this identity.

Transport strata, exact host/session identities, operational receipts, and raw tapes are private and `private_not_distributed`. The public study retains only reusable estimand and admission contracts. A passing side would provide prediction evidence only; an action requires a separate preregistration and private deployment approval.

The project-level [BABEL map](docs/babel_external_market_research_map.md) keeps the routes parallel rather than serial. P1 has completed its count gate but is stopped at the exact-lifecycle and row-level gates; independently, the Development-only [`external_adverse_quote_edge_guard_mechanics_v1`](docs/external_adverse_quote_edge_guard_mechanics_v1_spec_20260802.json) can inspect receive-time visibility, all/LOO direction, role support, outward quote movement and cap clipping without reading reward. Its denominator contains all paired quote decisions, not exact first-add decisions, so it cannot inherit P1 prediction evidence or register an F09 action.

The [Development mechanics report](docs/external_adverse_quote_edge_guard_mechanics_v1_development_20260802.md) retains this as clock-limited evidence. Seventeen windows over 16 days supplied 9,138 paired quote opportunities. There were 26 outward coordinate changes among 7,786 eligible opener/add side-opportunities; only six occurred on add quotes. The report also corrects a live-log semantic trap: `inventory_ratio` is an unsigned magnitude, so role is derived from a separately hash-bound, strictly-prior signed-position state journal. Exact decision time, two quote-log days, and authoritative order/ACK/queue paths remain unavailable.

That observed candidate rate is only 0.334%, versus the frozen 5% minimum in `action_execution_selective_v2`. A 0.5 randomized assignment would therefore change only about 13 paths. P2 v1 is retained as successful mechanics evidence, but it is not eligible for randomized action registration and no PnL was read.

The registered infrastructure successor, [`external_adverse_quote_edge_guard_exact_opener_mechanics_v2`](docs/external_adverse_quote_edge_guard_exact_opener_mechanics_v2_spec_20260802.json), adds stable decision IDs, exact decision/feature-ready clocks, signed inventory, baseline and shadow coordinates, and native submit/activation/cancel/fill lineage. Its formal denominator is exact opener opportunities, not P1 first-add. The collection-time [`v2.1 execution amendment`](docs/external_adverse_quote_edge_guard_exact_opener_mechanics_v2_1_20260802.md) now enforces an exact schema allowlist, stable non-null identifiers, unique strict lifecycle sequences, at most one terminal outcome per order, and exact baseline/candidate denominator rules. Operational fill and terminal events are read only for lifecycle mechanics; economic outcomes remain unread. The 5% support floor must pass separately for BUY and SELL, while the pooled rate is diagnostic only.

A historical [`collection preflight`](docs/external_adverse_quote_edge_guard_exact_opener_mechanics_v2_1_collection_preflight_failure_20260802.md) identified missing runtime hash binding, source/config fail-closed checks, writer-health admission, inherited-order quarantine, REST cancel-reject journaling, and atomic daily admission. The reusable [`v2.2 execution contract`](docs/external_adverse_quote_edge_guard_exact_opener_mechanics_v2_2_20260803.md) specifies those controls without asserting that any current runtime or host satisfies them. BUY/SELL support counting begins only after valid private admission. If either side remains below 5%, this guard closes for action support. Removing LOO or introducing a rank budget requires a separate future identity.

The separate Development-only [`cross_venue_causal_fair_price_v1`](docs/cross_venue_causal_fair_price_v1_spec_20260801.json) constructs a two-of-three Bitget/Bybit/OKX fair price from causal historical one-second individual-trade bars. It removes past-only venue basis, converts through USDCUSDT, uses a robust weighted median, and shrinks the local-to- external lead by an outcome-blind online gain. The all-venue and three true leave-one-venue-out variants share one common validity mask. Overall common support is 89.65%, but these provider-clock historical bars do not establish AWS receive-time live transport.

Its independent F09 action sibling, [`cross_venue_fair_center_shift_randomized_replay_v1.1`](../f09_campaign_action_uplift/docs/cross_venue_fair_center_shift_randomized_replay_v1_1_development_20260801.md), shifted the complete bid/ask pair while preserving spread and the rest of the strategy path. The 24-day Grade-A reward point estimate was positive, but its lower bound crossed zero and the leave-Bybit-out reward and USDC/day directions were negative. That action is closed on Development. This result does not modify or unlock the separately preregistered first-add receive-time M0/M1 identity.

Historical coordinate-parity work reproduced shift ticks, candidate prices, GTX clamps, and pair-spread preservation on its admitted sample. That closes implementation transport only, not economic transport or current deployment authority. Operational shadow/recorder identities and retirement receipts are private and not distributed.
