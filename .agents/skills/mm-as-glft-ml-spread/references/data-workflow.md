Use quality tied to the current files and parser. Historical `data_quality.py` exclusions or old supplier bad-day reports do not admit/exclude new inputs; consult them only for matching historical identities. Keep declared dates, unknown support and previous-use status. A known exchange interruption is not a recurring download obligation: retain measured gaps/staleness and unsupported outcomes.

Preserve purchased originals and separate derivatives. Honor the actual source policy; retained alternate downloaders do not grant fallback. Match venue/contract/symbol/units/channel, never silently substitute reference or spot markets.

Restore contiguous complete messages before atomic snapshots; no non-contiguous grouping or partial snapshot publication. Retain all known depth before Top-N and same-version BBO. Rebase is not market cancel flow or free queue reset. Missing native sequence/order/queue fields remain unavailable.

Separate source clock meaning, output time, observation age and modeled readiness. Provider receipt is provenance/grouping, not local strategy time or network calibration. Unit conversion does not prove clock origin. Carry only past valid state, retaining observation age; no future-fill or sampling-driven freshness.

Deduplicate by reliable market/trade identity and equal content; conflicting IDs fail, distinct IDs at one timestamp survive. Individual events, native packets and derived groups differ. Unknown quantities/counts cannot become zero; observed-empty is not proof of exchange-zero. Carried valuation prices are not new trades/OHLC.

For parser, observation or input-semantic changes, test affected atomic messages, chunk/shard invariance, cross-day context, conservation, identity, missing/stale and future visibility. Whole-calendar admission still needs actual daily evidence for that calendar; small fixtures are not full acceptance. Reuse applicable verified results rather than rescanning unchanged data for a documentation edit. Rebuild derivatives invalidated by source/schema changes and reject incompatible old-artifact fallback.

Output input/derivative identity, coverage/support, actual versus pending tests, changed consumers and blockers. Data-only work needs no quote decomposition or economic-gain claim.

## Transfer and backup route

Do not impose cumulative wall-time or per-account elapsed-time budgets on owner-authorized transfer, restoration or archival. A healthy operation must not stop merely because it has run for a fixed number of hours or minutes. A bounded scope means the explicit original files/accounts and authorized consumers, not an execution-time quota. Retain actual disk/OOM, identity/integrity, connection-establishment and demonstrated no-progress protections; diagnose real failures using process and byte/progress evidence rather than treating elapsed runtime alone as a failure. Preserve completed work and continue only missing original objects after a diagnosed interruption, without resetting a batch or duplicating writers.

When an actual transfer is needed, select the best feasible route for the affected account/version and destination, not separately for every file. Consult compatible verified copies on the destination, trusted peers, local storage, ORICO and Netdisk as relevant; reuse applicable location inventories and recent route measurements. Refresh them when the version, destination, availability or performance changes, evidence is stale, or a decisive uncertainty remains. Do not probe every host again without a reason, and do not use an old inventory as proof of current deletion safety. On the workstation explicitly check ORICO before regeneration; prefer destination reuse and the lowest total cost to verified availability, without assuming any route is always best.

Compare reuse/copy, peer transfer, direct SSH, Netdisk and compatible local generation where relevant. Count all network legs, validation/staging/cleanup cost, CPU/RAM/disk, stability, resumability, egress and interference with active jobs. Netdisk may require both upload and download; avoid an unnecessary workstation relay. Use a bounded whole-file trial only when existing evidence cannot decide, never duplicate a full batch as a benchmark. Generation must preserve the required input/cache identity; do not change expected hashes or silently regenerate cross-platform features to pass. Record the selected source/route and brief reason in the existing owner. On failure inspect alternatives before a bounded recovery; do not blindly retry. Network/key changes need existing authority; revoke temporary access afterward. Safely stop the old writer before switching, preserve partial work, prevent concurrent same-version/index writers and verify received files rather than trusting client exit alone.

Keep each existing data file whole with its original filename and directory structure. Do not introduce `.part` transport wrappers, generic `part-000001.parquet` shards or packing merely for transport/convenience. Use descriptive market/symbol/data-type/date names for new datasets. A concrete format limitation requiring splitting needs explanation and approval first; size alone does not establish it. Provider-internal multipart is acceptable when the stored result is one normal whole file. Meaningful existing shards and unfinished atomic construction directories are not disposable transport wrappers; identify contents and consumers before any approved consolidation or renaming.

## Working-cache lifecycle

The owner's 2026-10-19 instruction cancels mandatory Netdisk backup of prepare caches. Retain the verified original ORICO version; after the last actual consumer exits and dependencies/open files are clear, delete the execution-host working copy without uploading to or waiting for Netdisk. Reuse an existing ORICO copy, do not copy it again. Newly generated reusable caches missing from ORICO may be retained there before working-copy retirement; no Netdisk upload is authorized by this lifecycle. For cache deduplication and transfer acceptance compare the account/date coverage and version, exact relative file list and each file's byte size; do not recompute cache SHA. This is size-based acceptance, not cryptographic equality. Formal results, models and purchased originals keep their separate retention requirements. Use one existing location/coverage index, not a duplicate archive/catalog.

Replay from the execution host's local working disk, not external storage. Stage only current/near-term account versions, verify the above acceptance and bind the local directory. Reuse one compatible read-only market prepare across consumers; account/order/inventory state stays independent. No per-arm copies, whole-calendar prefill, new hardlinks or symlinks. Reserve space for active workers, outputs and the system; insufficient capacity means waiting, not lowering protection or changing active paths.

The lifecycle is retained archive -> bounded local staging -> accepted replay -> immediate working-copy removal. After the last actual consumer's complete economics/results are accepted and it exits, check retained copies, active/queued/recovery or pinned dependencies and open files; remove only disposable copies and retire availability in the existing index. Do not wait 14 days, for the whole experiment or disk exhaustion. Historical unused caches need the same checks; age is neither a waiting period nor deletion authority. Queued-only copies may be evicted only with the exact retained archive and the original restoration binding/path verified. Otherwise retain the affected object and state the missing dependency; UNKNOWN is not a blanket stop for unrelated cleanup. Preserve archives, original observations/timelines, formal results, purchases and unique evidence. Explicit retirement of obsolete evidence remains a separately scoped owner action.

Before a new executor/batch, identify the responsible owner and actual staging/post-consumer maintenance entry point. This does not require a dedicated cleanup daemon, a new receipt format or rerunning historical failures. If the frozen runner lacks cleanup, use an existing authorized bounded maintenance path at completed-consumer boundaries; do not hot-edit it, restart healthy workers or create an unauthorized recurring automation. Record actual cleanup or concrete retention blockers in the existing owner receipt and update location/file inventories. Editing instructions does not install automatic cleanup.

## Lock roles

Use per-file identity locks during transfers, bounded upload concurrency and receiver verification. Runtime coordination locks are not payloads: filter them from backups/transfers/archives and both inventories, even if an old manifest lists them or they contain PID/owner records. Do not recreate, transfer or retry missing runtime locks; their absence is not missing data when payloads and required metadata match. Remove archived runtime-lock copies only at inactive locations during authorized cleanup. Never delete a lock in use or disable runtime locking. Classify by purpose, not a blanket `*.lock` exclusion: retain dependency lockfiles (`requirements.lock`, `uv.lock`, `poetry.lock`, `Pipfile.lock`, `yarn.lock`, `Cargo.lock`), required metadata and unknown-role files until identified.
