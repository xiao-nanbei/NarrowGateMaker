## Scope and authority

Historical closed/exhausted/promotion decisions do not block new owner-authorized question-specific experiments. Preserve historical identities, defect regression tests and previous-use; do not relabel old artifacts as new results. Protect actual active jobs and frozen releases; resolve their state from the relevant owner, execution records and current research index, not phase descriptions in instructions. The research plan does not itself authorize live operations or publication.

Under the owner's 2026-10-17 instruction, do not impose backtest-count, candidate-count, total-replay or consumed-compute quotas. Run the evaluations and engineering/recovery checks needed by the requested research; historical budget exhaustion is not a reason to refuse or stop them. Planned account totals describe coverage, not spendable allowance or admission caps. Continue validating the declared question, calendar, model/account identity, comparable coverage and previous-use, and prevent accidental duplicate submission or blind retry. Resource/concurrency protections, execution-host schedules, external-service limits and strategy risk constraints are separate from backtest quotas and remain governed by their actual contracts. Do not rewrite frozen historical records or hot-edit active releases to remove an old guard; remove quotas from maintained/new admission paths and preserve provenance for any necessary rerun.

These instructions apply to the checkout containing this file, not to another remote's release. Read the actual branch, commit/tree and dirty state; preserve unrelated edits and active jobs. A published copy governs only its own tree. Never infer a repository's visibility from its name.

Current dataset scope, source policy and migration status are defined once in [data/dataset_scope.json](data/dataset_scope.json). For experiment execution, evaluation, behavior changes or an identified frozen dependency, read the selected experiment's actual plan and relevant frozen manifests for splits, model/calibration policy, baseline, latency and accounting. Routine code, test or wording maintenance does not require unrelated experiment records. A policy change is not proof of implemented consumers or completed validation. Do not extend dates with today's clock or copy historical exclusions into a new source identity.

## Change-scoped evidence and validation

Reduce unrelated reading and repeated work, not decisive evidence. Start from the actual diff, surrounding logic and affected consumers; expand when units, clocks, defaults, loading, shared state, persistence or failure behavior remain material and unclear. Names, remembered summaries, truncated searches and documentation claims are not implementation proof. Preserve source locators and unresolved limits; do not guess to meet a token, file or test limit.

Validate the requested change. Prose and instruction edits need meaning, precedence, metadata, relevant links and diff checks, not model loading or business replay. Code changes need relevant existing regressions; expand for concrete shared-interface, concurrency, persistence, packaging or failure effects. Reuse a locatable successful result only while its relevant inputs and environment still match. Report the actual command/status and relevant failures, warnings, skips and zero collection; test count is neither target nor cap. No positive-PnL requirement applies to behavior-preserving engineering.

Do not turn an implementation detail into an admission gate. A current blocking check needs a concrete protected invariant, the affected object and a failure action appropriate to this operation. Do not require historical failure directories, particular launcher/test filenames, fixed patch shapes, removed run-count quotas, or a newly spawned housekeeping daemon. Preserve declared scientific coverage: accepting a valid batch is different from declaring the complete experiment finished. Historical count/split/identity constraints stay with their original experiments.

A check not performed is not a PASS: distinguish provenance-only metadata from verified runtime acceptance. Static source/model/result acceptance may be reused for unchanged immutable objects within its verified trust scope. An old PASS, matching path, or copied mtime is not sufficient. Do not remove per-event causality, identity-conflict, quantity, order-state, duplicate-write or runtime-risk checks by calling them static. Refresh process, writer, dependency and target-capacity facts at consequential boundaries. Match prepare-transfer checks to the owner's name/size policy without calling that cryptographic equality; do not extend it to formal results or models.

Keep one implementation of a shared check at its existing owner. Combine redundant traversals without changing coverage or persistence semantics. Frozen releases and original receipts stay unchanged; modify maintained/new entry points. Already-fixed items require only applicable targeted confirmation, not reimplementation. Production code must not extract executable functions from tests or depend on obsolete one-off scripts; migrate real consumers before retiring disposable copies.

Distinguish queue wait, active progress, connection failure, stalled work and an explicitly authorized deadline. Do not invent numerical admission limits. Separate compute completion, validation, transfer and cleanup; resume a failed post-compute stage under its original identity instead of rerunning economics. Use existing state/CLI interfaces, with idempotence and receiver reconciliation; never blindly retry UNKNOWN remote effects. Shared-resource hazards still affect every genuinely dependent operation.

Deletion/moves require scoped imports, CLI/dynamic/configuration and frozen/active/queued/held/recovery/pinned dependencies, retained objects and live-use checks. State consumer -> object -> purpose. Resolve the affected edge or retain that object, not the whole project. Update findings when code or dependencies change. A prior no-reader check cannot prevent a later reader: coordinate through the existing owner before deleting. Preserve originals, unique results, required backups and the latest necessary recovery point.

Stop once the requested changes and their affected checks are supported. Do not create a new gate registry, certificate chain, approval process, daemon, audit-report series or permanent one-off runner. Use normal diffs, existing acceptance and concise task results. The guidance does not itself disable runtime validators or grant training, replay, trading, cloud or publication authority.

## Development and ownership

Prefer a local PoolRun master for workstation-led research so stopping remote compute hosts does not remove the controller. Preserve one authoritative master and existing task/Attempt identities during migration; stop the old master before starting its replacement. Remote Agents still require an approved reachable control endpoint.

For changes to an existing experiment's concurrency, arm order, execution location or log path, prefer existing entry-point arguments, scheduler configuration or direct commands. If a one-off launcher is necessary, scope it to that task and delete it after completion; do not automatically promote it to a research artifact or require long-term archival.

At task closure, delete disposable inventories, directory trees and scratch summaries whose purpose is complete. Preserve requested deliverables, unique results and active dependencies; do not create an archive merely to retain temporary housekeeping files.

Under the owner's 2026-10-01 instruction, each code repository's published `main` must contain exactly one root commit representing the current complete source tree. At each completed delivery, consolidate authorized changes into that root and push the identical commit to both established remotes using explicit expected-tip force-with-lease. This is not one additional commit per task. Preserve a private recovery bundle and immutable experiment identities; never relabel historical evidence. Delete local and remote development branches only after proving their work is integrated; preserve unique unmerged work and checked-out worktree contents. Later explicit no-push instructions override this default. Read the publication protocol for the exact scope, concurrency checks and CI controls; never use blind force, publish private artifacts or activate trading as a consequence of publication.

For new local interactive work, use the owner's standalone Python 3.12 at `~/.local/python3.12/bin/python3` (the default `python3` in new shells), without virtual-environment activation. Use `python3 -m pip`, `python3 -m pytest` and `python3 -m ruff` so tools use that interpreter. Check the actual version and required imports before work. Preserve interpreter bindings of active processes and frozen releases; do not migrate or restart them merely to normalize environments. Remote approved environments remain separately bound. Select tests under the evidence rule above, with applicable lint and `git diff --check`. Keep long local macOS jobs under `caffeinate`; remote jobs need their own durable runner.

Keep hosted CI on Linux runners, matching the production target and the owner's cost preference. Do not add macOS/Windows runners or cross-platform matrices without explicit authorization. Local macOS checks remain allowed; this does not authorize dispatching hosted CI.

Test doubles must implement the supported production interface. Dependency injection is encouraged; do not add alternative business-method protocols just to accommodate an obsolete fixture. Retired business interfaces must fail rather than be normalized at startup. Artifact conversion is an explicit one-time offline operation outside the application; retain real assembly and rejection tests.

Read [module ownership](docs/architecture.md) before relocating code. Reuse maintained modules rather than creating parallel frameworks or one-off runners. Acquisition adapters belong to `data/downloaders/` when that migration exists in the actual checkout; facts/observations belong to `data/`, shared features to `features/`, runtime contracts to `narrowgate/runtime/`, family-specific research to its `research/families/` owner. Existing `models/audit/` locations are transitional, not a default destination for all new diagnostics. Verify imports and actual CLI help before recommending commands. Do not recreate removed paths.

No project symlinks, supplier aliases, duplicate raw trees or placeholder data. Keep raw and derived directories physically separate. Before cleanup verify replacement, active users and index references; age alone does not authorize deletion. Preserve purchased originals, unique edits and frozen research/live evidence.

Before substantial batches, check actual resource health and bounded per-shard disk/RAM, including concurrent jobs. ## Documentation, privacy and results

Under the owner's 2026-10-20 instruction, assign every new B0 baseline version a permanent, unique `B0_YYYYMMDD_NN` display identifier before reporting or using it as a comparison baseline. For new versions, use the version-freeze date in Asia/Shanghai and the next unused sequence for that date; never reuse an identifier. Bind it in the owning experiment's existing records to the exact original baseline/arm, parameters and frozen source identity. Changes to the baseline's parameters or frozen implementation require a new identifier; reruns, report refreshes, storage moves and display-only edits of the same version do not. Include the identifier in human-facing comparison tables and receipts, along with independent-versus-continuous account semantics, coverage and statistical cut. Retain original machine identities and immutable historical evidence; historical display aliases use an evidenced original version date, never an invented timestamp. Unknown historical versions must be marked unresolved rather than silently mapped to a known B0. This naming rule does not authorize a new baseline, replay or parameter change.

Read [path conventions](docs/path_conventions.md) when changing paths/locators and the [public/private contract](docs/public_private_documentation_contract.md) when privacy, evidence availability, external presentation or publication is affected. A wording-only edit does not trigger unrelated catalog audits. Agent instructions stay English; materially changed maintained human guides retain their bilingual pair and synchronization dates. Keep prose paragraphs on one physical line. Do not upload purchased data, credentials, private locators, models or sealed results with source.

Report what this task changed, tested and left incomplete. Quote analysis needs units/clocks; a downloader or directory repair does not need a quote decomposition. Use one run identity for source tree, configuration, data/model roots and output receipt; do not proliferate leaf hashes across prose, code and tests. Source identity need not be public: preserve an exact local/private commit or immutable source snapshot and explicitly bind any authorized overlay.
